Approximate Nutrition for Common Foods (per typical serving)

Apple, medium (~182 g): ~95 kcal; protein 0.5 g; carbs 25 g; fat 0.3 g. Fiber ~4 g. Good snack alternative to sweets.

Banana, medium (~118 g): ~105 kcal; protein 1.3 g; carbs 27 g; fat 0.4 g. Useful pre-workout carb source.

Boiled egg, large: ~70–80 kcal; protein 6–7 g; carbs 0.5 g; fat 5 g. Complete protein with vitamins.

Chicken breast, cooked 100 g: ~165 kcal; protein 31 g; carbs 0 g; fat 3.6 g. Lean high-protein option.

Brown rice, cooked 1 cup (~195 g): ~215 kcal; protein 5 g; carbs 45 g; fat 1.6 g. More fiber than white rice.

White rice, cooked 1 cup: ~200 kcal; protein 4 g; carbs 45 g; fat 0.4 g. Easy to digest; pair with protein and vegetables.

Oats, dry 40 g (about 1/2 cup): ~150 kcal; protein 5 g; carbs 27 g; fat 3 g. Excellent breakfast base.

Greek yogurt, plain nonfat 170 g: ~100 kcal; protein 17 g; carbs 6 g; fat 0–1 g. High protein snack.

Paneer, 100 g: ~265 kcal; protein 18 g; carbs 1–3 g; fat 20 g. Rich in protein and fat; portion carefully.

Dal (cooked lentils), 1 cup: ~230 kcal; protein 18 g; carbs 40 g; fat 1 g. Plant protein staple.

Almonds, 28 g (about 23 nuts): ~160 kcal; protein 6 g; carbs 6 g; fat 14 g. Healthy fats; watch portions.

Avocado, half medium: ~120 kcal; protein 1.5 g; carbs 6 g; fat 11 g. Mostly unsaturated fat.

Salmon, cooked 100 g: ~200 kcal; protein 22 g; carbs 0 g; fat 13 g. Omega-3 rich.

Chapati / whole wheat roti, 1 medium: ~100–120 kcal; protein 3–4 g; carbs 18–22 g; fat 2–3 g depending on oil.

Potato, boiled medium: ~130 kcal; protein 3 g; carbs 30 g; fat 0.1 g. Potassium-rich; prefer boiled/baked over deep-fried.

Values are approximate educational estimates and vary by brand, cooking method, and portion size.
