Daily Meal Planning Guidelines

Build meals around protein + fiber + colorful plants. This supports fullness, stable energy, and micronutrient intake.

Sample balanced day (adjust portions to calorie needs):

Breakfast: Oats cooked in milk/water with banana and a spoon of peanut butter; or vegetable omelette with 1–2 rotis and fruit.

Mid-morning: Fruit (apple/orange) or yogurt, or a small handful of nuts.

Lunch: 1–2 rotis or 1 cup rice/millets + dal or chicken/fish/paneer/tofu + large salad or cooked vegetables + curd.

Evening snack: Roasted chana, sprouts chaat, or hummus with cucumber/carrot sticks.

Dinner: Lighter than lunch if preferred — grilled protein, vegetables, and a modest carb portion; finish 2–3 hours before sleep when possible.

Meal combinations that work well:
- Dal + rice + salad
- Grilled fish + quinoa + greens
- Paneer/tofu stir-fry + millet roti
- Chicken breast + sweet potato + broccoli
- Greek yogurt bowl + berries + seeds

Special considerations:
- Vegetarian: combine legumes + grains across the day for amino acid variety.
- Weight loss: prioritize protein and vegetables; reduce fried foods and sugary drinks.
- Muscle gain: raise protein and total calories; include post-workout carbs + protein.
- Diabetes-friendly patterns: emphasize fiber, non-starchy vegetables, and consistent meal timing; limit sugary drinks.

This is educational nutrition guidance, not personalized medical advice. People with medical conditions should consult a clinician or registered dietitian.
