Macronutrients Basics for Everyday Nutrition

Calories are a measure of food energy. Adult daily needs often range from about 1,600 to 3,000 kcal depending on age, sex, body size, and activity.

Protein builds and repairs tissues, supports immune function, and helps satiety. Common sources: eggs, fish, chicken, lentils, beans, Greek yogurt, tofu, paneer, and lean meats. A typical target is about 0.8–1.6 g protein per kg body weight for most healthy adults.

Carbohydrates are the body's preferred fuel. Prefer complex carbs: oats, brown rice, quinoa, whole wheat, millets, sweet potato, fruits, and vegetables. Limit added sugars and refined flours when possible.

Fats are essential for hormones and fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). Prefer unsaturated fats from nuts, seeds, avocado, olive oil, and fatty fish. Limit excess saturated fat and avoid industrial trans fats.

A balanced plate often looks like: half vegetables/fruit, one quarter protein, one quarter whole grains or starchy vegetables, plus a modest healthy fat.

Hydration matters. Water, unsweetened tea, and milk can support daily fluid needs. Sugary drinks add calories with little nutrition.
