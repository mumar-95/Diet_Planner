Healthy Food Alternatives and Swaps

Instead of deep-fried snacks (samosa, chips), choose roasted chickpeas, air-fried vegetables, or a handful of nuts.

Instead of sugary soda, choose sparkling water with lemon, unsweetened iced tea, or buttermilk (chaas).

Instead of white bread sandwiches, use whole-grain bread or wrap fillings in lettuce/roti with lean protein and vegetables.

Instead of cream-heavy curries, use tomato-onion gravy with yogurt or a small amount of milk, and load vegetables.

Instead of fried chicken, prefer grilled, baked, or air-fried chicken with herbs and spices.

Instead of sugary breakfast cereal, choose oats with fruit and nuts, or eggs with vegetables.

Instead of ice cream every day, try frozen yogurt, fruit smoothie bowls, or frozen banana "nice cream".

Instead of mayonnaise-heavy salads, use Greek yogurt, hummus, mustard, or olive oil + lemon dressing.

Instead of refined white rice at every meal, rotate brown rice, quinoa, millets (ragi, jowar, bajra), or cauliflower rice mixes.

Instead of candy bars, choose dark chocolate (small piece), dates with nuts, or fresh fruit.

Portion awareness: even healthy foods are calorie-dense (nuts, oils, cheese, avocado). Measure occasionally.

Cooking methods: steaming, boiling, grilling, baking, and sautéing with little oil are generally healthier than deep frying.
